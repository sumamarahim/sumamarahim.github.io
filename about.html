Academic Journey

I am currently in my 5th semester of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program at National Skills University, Islamabad, Pakistan, maintaining a cumulative GPA of 3.37 / 4.00.

Rather than confining my academic curiosity to a single narrow domain early in my career, I actively cultivate a broad foundation across multiple computing disciplines—including software engineering, networks, cybersecurity, machine learning, and data systems. This interdisciplinary stance allows me to approach problems with flexibility, synthesize ideas across domain boundaries, and quickly adapt to specialized research requirements.
